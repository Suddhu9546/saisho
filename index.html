About Us

Financial operations, handled with structure and accuracy

Saisho Pecuniaria Ishi Private Limited is an Ahmedabad-based company focused on accounting, taxation, financial management, compliance, and advisory services. Led by Manthan Joshi, CEO & Director — a Finance Strategist and Tax Specialist (MBA – Finance) — the company brings structure, accuracy, and clarity to clients' financial operations.

From day-to-day bookkeeping and ledger management to taxation, financial statements, and accounts payable and receivable, our focus is on helping clients maintain dependable financial records and make better-informed decisions. Our approach combines financial discipline, attention to detail, and practical business understanding — across routine accounting needs and broader financial and compliance requirements.
